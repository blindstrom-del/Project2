# Product Website — Design Guidelines

## 1. Brand & Creative Direction

Brand Name: Being
Product: Hair Care Product line with products specifically for all different hair types.
Target Audience: This brand caters to all people with all hair types.

### Visual Direction
Describe the overall visual direction of your website. As you open the website you see the top of the page with the logo, the navigation and a wheel of products that are rotating between the different products with a tag line " care for every hair" As you start to scroll down, the different hair types are presented with a line drawing for the texture of the hair and the style of hair is typed out for clarification. Beneath the title of each type of hair, there are three products listed with a button called "mine!" that instructs the customer to click if they have found their hair type. After they click, the page extends to show the 3 steps of the hair line, with buttons to add each item to the cart, with another button option in the bottom right stating the addition of all three products for a discount. The brand personality is fun and caters to all individuals. 

Consider brand personality, visual style,
photography, typography and composition.

## 4. UI Components

### Navigation
Describe your desktop and mobile navigation.
The desktop and mobile navigation both have a nav bar which is illustrated lines representing the different hair types that sits in the top right corner of the page. 

### Buttons
Describe your primary and secondary buttons. The primary button is the "mine!" button that is in the shape of the icon. The secondary buttons are the "add to cart" oval buttons as well as the shopping cart button that has the 20% off around it. 

### Product Cards
Describe the layout and appearance of
your product cards. The product cards are listed under each type of hair with a translucent background. 

### Featured Products
Describe how products are organized
and displayed.
The products are shown in a row of three on the desktop version and are shown in a slideshow in the mobile version.

### Other UI Elements
Describe additional reusable UI elements.
The pattern of the red icon is used at the end of each section with more information about the steps of each style of hair. The backgrounds change color depending on the style of hair you are looking at, underneath the line drawing
of the hair type. The products are listed repeatedly in each section of hair. The buttons are repeated in each section under each step of the hair line and the "buy the bundle" button is also repeated at the bottom of each section.

## 5. Digital Experience

Describe the intended digital experience
in the first section of your homepage.
The digital expierience is the wheel of products that are cycling through the wheel to the center with the tag line above. As you continue to scroll down, you discover the specific hair types. 

What should visitors see?
They are seeing the fun color pallet the line illustrations of the hair types and the animation of the wheel of products.

What makes this experience unique to
a digital medium?

It is simple but still fun without being overcrowded.

What should happen when visitors interact?
When visitors choose to hit the button of their hair choice, the page lengthens for more information about their hair care steps. 

Describe any planned animations,
transitions, hover effects, scrolling
or interactive behavior.
The buttons have a before and after click, the wheel of products changes in rotation of what product is in the center.
